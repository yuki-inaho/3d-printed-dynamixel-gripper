# JST EH Connector Reference

Retrieved 2026-09-23 from [JST's public catalog](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eEH.pdf).
Local `eEH.pdf` SHA256: `9e35874bf27505bab79d6af783118fa926ceef7a91b7cb8421f108ff69a1b7f2`.
Pages2/3 were rendered and visually inspected. This is reference material, not
permission to redistribute supplier CAD or a purchased-component inspection.

- EHR-3:2.5 mm pitch,9.5 mm width,3.8 mm thickness. The drawing labels6.5 mm body
  height and a separate0.6 mm projection; do not assume a6.5 mm total envelope.
- B3B-EH header:10 mm width,6 mm above the PCB and3.2 mm pins; assembly drawing
  gives8.1 mm nominal mated height. Tolerances/actual seating still matter.
- The [ROBOTIS XL430 manual](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/dxl/x/xl430-w250/)
  specifies EHR-03 housing, B3B-EH-A header and SEH-001T-P0.6 contact.
- ROBOTIS lists21 AWG while JST's catalog contact range ends at22 AWG. Do not
  silently approve a self-crimped combination or infer E148/system ratings.
